How It Works

Simple. Local. Student-Powered.

Whether you're buying or selling, CampusKala works entirely within your university — verified, trusted, and community-first.

🛒

For Buyers

1
Sign up with your .edu email
Only Christ University students can join. Your college email verifies your identity — no imposters, no outsiders.
2
Browse listings by category
Filter by products, services, events, or free giveaways. Search for exactly what you need — from notes to noodles.
3
Connect & pick up on campus
Message the seller directly. Arrange pickup at the food court, library, or hostel — all within campus.
🏪

For Sellers

1
Create your student storefront
Set up your shop profile in minutes — add a bio, your department, and what you're selling or offering.
2
List products or services
Post your handmade items, second-hand goods, skill-based services, or recurring offerings — keep your stall open all year.
3
Earn, grow, and build your brand
Get reviews, build a loyal customer base among your peers, and grow your entrepreneurship journey right from your hostel room.

Why CampusKala

The Stall That Never
Packs Up

🔒

University-Verified Only

Every buyer and seller is a verified Christ University student. No strangers, no spam — a truly trusted campus circle.

📅

Always Open, No Fest Needed

College stalls last 3 days. Your CampusKala shop runs all 365 — even during exams, holidays, and sem breaks.

💸

Zero Platform Fees

We take nothing. 100% of what buyers pay goes directly to the student seller. We believe in peer-to-peer, not middlemen.

🤝

Hyperlocal Pickup

No shipping drama. Meet at the campus cafeteria, hostel block, or library gate. Campus is your delivery zone.

⭐

Peer Reviews & Ratings

Build trust with genuine ratings from classmates. Good reviews help student businesses grow their campus reputation.

📣

Fest Announcements

When Blossoms or Spectrum happens, sellers can activate their physical stall mode and broadcast deals to their existing follower base.
